Press & Media

A selection of articles and interviews exploring my research on Ehlers-Danlos syndromes (EDS) and Hypermobility Spectrum Disorders (HSD), the challenges surrounding diagnosis, and the perspectives of patient-researchers.

CNN · Article · 24 Dec 2022

“Millions have the same ‘bendy body’ disease as my daughter. Why isn’t the medical profession paying more attention?” ↗

A feature exploring the under-recognition of hEDS and the challenges surrounding diagnosis. The article highlights my research proposing a novel mechanism linking collagen-cell interactions to connective tissue dysfunction, and its potential to inform future diagnostic approaches.

CNN Health · Video · 16 Dec 2022

“'We're born with this and will never be free:' Hear stories from people with Ehlers-Danlos syndrome” ↗

A video feature sharing the experiences of people living with hEDS, highlighting the impact of the condition on daily life and the challenges surrounding recognition and diagnosis. I contribute my perspective as both a person living with hEDS and a biomedical researcher working to improve understanding of the condition.

STAT News · Article · 12 Dec 2022

“Revenge of the gaslit patients: Now, as scientists, they’re tackling Ehlers-Danlos syndromes” ↗

A feature exploring how researchers are addressing gaps in the understanding and diagnosis of hEDS. The article discusses my research on the limitations of the Beighton Score and my proposed mechanistic framework linking altered cell-matrix interactions and fibroblast mechanobiology to connective tissue dysfunction.

The Rest Room · Podcast · 23 Nov 2022

“What happens when diagnostic criteria aren't fit for purpose?” ↗

In this conversation with Natasha Lipman, I discuss the history and limitations of the Beighton Score, challenges surrounding the hEDS diagnostic criteria, and the search for genetic causes of hEDS. I also talk about my experimental research and proposed disease mechanism, exploring how altered cell-matrix interactions and cellular mechanics may contribute to connective tissue dysfunction.

Dr Teeroumanee Nadan · Video · 16 Mar 2022

“Celebrating small steps in HE – Diversity in STEM, Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome research ft. Sabeeha Malek” ↗

In this interview, I discuss how my experience of living with hEDS informed my early research, including my critique of the Beighton Score and my PhD work developing a proposed mechanobiological explanation for connective tissue dysfunction. We also explore the value of patient-researchers, the importance of supporting diversity in STEM, and how lived experience can help bring overlooked scientific questions to light.

Massage & Fitness Magazine · Article · 25 Sep 2021

“Is the Beighton Score for Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome valid or just BS?” ↗

An interview discussing my research questioning the validity of the Beighton Score as a measure of generalised joint hypermobility. The feature explores the distinction between reliability and validity, factors that can influence joint mobility, and why clinicians should consider the wider clinical picture rather than relying on the score alone to exclude generalised joint hypermobility.
